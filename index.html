How this system is trying to win

Farzad-style monitor surface: explain edge, enforce guardrails, and show what changed.

The edge being pursued

  • Find company-specific change before the move is fully priced.
  • Use AI for ranking and explanation, not for waiving risk rules.
  • Prefer liquid names with clean exits and clear catalysts.

Decision architecture

  • Alpha Radar creates candidates.
  • Two models review independently.
  • Deterministic code validates sizing, stops, and reward/risk.
Hard limits
  • Minimum reward/risk: 1.5:1
  • Max single position: $200
  • Daily loss halt: $15 or 3%
  • Portfolio drawdown halt: 8%
  • Research budget cap: 30 calls / day
