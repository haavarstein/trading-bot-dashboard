How Hermes is trying to win

The edge it is pursuing, the limits it cannot waive, and how it acts without a live IBKR connection yet.

The edge it is pursuing

  • Broad-market discovery ranks catalyst/news setups before the 15-minute decision loop.
  • Dual decision paths must agree on action and symbol before risk code runs.
  • Local paper broker is the account source of truth: cash, fills, holdings, realized/open P/L.
  • Stops and targets are attached on entry; exits fire on stop, target, or rotation.
  • IBKR MCP stays paused until the personal paper account is approved/active.
